---
title: "Backdrop of Blue"
permalink: "/backdrop-of-blue/"
date: 2024-09-17T19:56:00+02:00
---

To barely make it up a hill on your bike  
And for a split-second feel you've been reborn  
Is merely to look at life through twisted thorns  
Like the flightless bird looks up at the red kite  

Man thinks thoughts and metaphors, very manlike  
The universe, indifferent, in sigh and yawn  
Stares back blankly like a silent foghorn  
Waiting for man's thoughts to finally subside  

Yet for a moment I rolled on feeling hope  
Fleeting, slippery hope, that one can't hold on to  
Like trying too hard in a game of skip-rope  

I thought of the bird above me, how it flew  
No thinking, no trying, and no secret oaths  
Just flapping its wings in a backdrop of blue
